1. Privacy at a glance

General information

The following gives a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you use this app. Personal data is any data that can be used to personally identify you. Detailed information can be found in the privacy policy below this text.

Data collection when using this app

Who is responsible for data collection?

Data processing is carried out by the operator of the app. Their contact details can be found in the section "Notice regarding the responsible party" in this privacy policy.

How do we collect your data?

Your data is collected, on the one hand, by you providing it to us, e.g. by creating a user account (email address) or uploading your own audio files. Other data is collected automatically by our IT systems or the service providers used when you access the app (e.g. technical data such as browser type or time of access), as well as — provided you have given consent — by embedded advertising services.

What do we use your data for?

Part of the data is collected to ensure the error-free provision of the app and cross-device synchronisation of your categories and jingles. Provided you have consented, data is also used to display advertising.

What rights do you have regarding your data?

You have the right at any time to receive free information about the origin, recipient and purpose of your stored personal data. You also have a right to request the correction or deletion of this data. If you have given consent to data processing, you can revoke this consent at any time with future effect. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the responsible supervisory authority.

4. Registration and user account

If you register for a user account in order to synchronise your categories and jingles across devices, we collect your email address (for login via magic link, without a password) as well as the categories, jingle names, trim markers you create, and the audio files you upload. This data is required in order to provide you with the app's core function (storage and synchronisation of your jingles) (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

Deleted jingles are kept in a trash for 24 hours and then permanently deleted. Your account data remains with us until you delete your account.

Without a user account, you can continue to use the app fully locally on your device; in this case, no data is transmitted to us.

5. Cookies and local storage

This app uses technically necessary local storage (including IndexedDB and local storage of your login session) so that the app also works fully offline and so that you do not need to sign in again every time you open it. This storage takes place exclusively on your device and/or serves the technical provision of the function you use (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR or Sec. 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG) and does not require separate consent.

Your decision in the cookie consent banner (acceptance/rejection of advertising) is also stored locally so that the banner does not reappear on every visit.

Cookies used for displaying advertising are described separately in section 7 and are only set following your express consent.

6. Server log files

Our hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser automatically transmits to us: browser type and version, operating system used, referrer URL, time of the server request, IP address. This data is not merged with other data sources. Collection is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the technically error-free provision of the app).
